## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Consistency is Key:** Try to practice every day, even if just for 10 minutes
2. **Review Regularly:** Don't skip your review sessions - they're crucial for retention
3. **Use New Words:** Try to use newly learned words in your own sentences
4. **Don't Fear Mistakes:** Mistakes are how we learn!
5. **Track Progress:** Update this file after each lesson to see your improvement
6. **Mix It Up:** Alternate between different lesson types to stay engaged
7. **Set Realistic Goals:** Start small and build up gradually
8. **Immerse Yourself:** Try to expose yourself to English outside of lessons too
